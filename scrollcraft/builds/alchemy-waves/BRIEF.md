# Alchemy & Waves Group Ltd — BRIEF

Interviewed across three rounds before any asset was generated. Answers are the
user's, condensed only where they typed in fragments.

## The eight answers

**1. Vibe, three to five words + references.**
Renaissance art fused with modern technology. Ancient knowledge × modern tech.
References given: Michelangelo's *Creation of Adam*; the Sistine ceiling;
Shopify Editions Winter 2026 (for scroll feeling, not look). Explicitly
**rejected** pop culture after first proposing it: *"I want fusion, but not pop
culture."*

**2. The scroll journey, in their words.**
Not specified section by section. The user delegated sequence and asked for a
holding-group structure with abstract pillars: *"Keep divisions abstract."*

**3. The energy curve.**
Not asked directly. Inferred from the two decisions that carry it: *sparse and
deliberate* art density, and *aphoristic* copy. Both mean restraint, silence,
few words held long.

**4. How it should feel, and the ONE moment.**
See the feeling curve below. The peak was chosen from the image the user kept
returning to across every round: two hands not quite touching, gold light in the
gap, the ancient world under one and the modern world under the other.

**5. One thing no site they have seen does.**
Answered by selection: *"The seam opens under the cursor."* The boundary between
the ancient and modern worlds responds to where the visitor is looking.

**6. How far from premium-minimal.**
Premium-minimal, hard. *"Sparse and deliberate"* — roughly five images total,
each given enormous room. Awwwards-level, *"no cheap animations, no clutter, no
generic AI landing page look."*

**7. One unbroken world, or distinct scenes?**
**One unbroken world.** The user asked for a single cinematic sequence scrubbed
by scroll, and the final asset is one continuous 10s shot with no cuts.

**8. What assets do they already have?**
Now: one Michelangelo-style painting and one 10-second film generated from it,
both approved, both in `public/media`. At interview time: nothing.

## The feeling curve

Written before the acts were rescored. One line per act: the emotion, then what
on screen causes it.

```
1  Awe          the painting arrives full-bleed and moves under the reader's own hand
2  Recognition  four lines, low and left, naming the thought they have already had
3  Stillness    the film stops. One paragraph resolving word by word on a plain ground
4  Weight       three disciplines, one enormous numeral at a time, nothing else moving
5  Complicity   THE PEAK. The seam answers the pointer. The page is looking back
6  Competence   a plain numbered index. The only act that reads rather than moves
7  Resolve      the mark at full size, one address, and it stops
```

No two adjacent acts share a feeling. Act 3 (Stillness) is the authored silence
placed directly before the peak, so Complicity has something to be a change from.

## The peak

**Act 5, The Seam.** As the sentence a visitor would say to a friend:

> you move your mouse and the painting takes a side — drift left and it warms
> into the desert, drift right and it goes cold and electric, and you realise
> the page has been watching where you look

It gets the three things the peak is owed:

- **The asset budget** — the only pointer-reactive treatment on the page, and
  the only place `--signal` appears at full strength.
- **The silence before it** — Act 4 (Pillars) moves one numeral at a time and
  nothing else. Act 3 is a static paragraph. Two quiet acts feeding one loud one.
- **The most scroll room** — the largest span on the page by a visible margin.

## The tell-someone sentence

> It's the site where the seam between the old world and the new one opens under
> your cursor, and the painting takes a side depending on where you are looking.

An experience, not a device name.

## Authored silence

Two passages are deliberately quiet and must not be read as dead scroll by the
verification pass:

1. **Between hero beats.** Each of the four lines clears completely before the
   next arrives. The gaps are silence, and the scrim lifts in them so the
   painting is seen unobstructed.
2. **Act 3, the Thesis.** A single paragraph on a plain ground with no media and
   no movement except the word ramp. This is the runway to the peak.
